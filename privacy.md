# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [May 13, 2026]_

This Privacy Policy describes how **Gross Out Diet** ("we", "us", "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information we collect

**Stored on your device only:**
- Food logs, cravings, Resist Points, streaks, and app settings.

**Processed when you use the G.O. Coach:**
- The text and any image you send to the coach is transmitted to our AI provider to generate a response. We do not store these messages on our servers.

**Collected by third parties:**
- **Apple App Store / Google Play:** purchase and subscription receipts.
- **RevenueCat:** subscription status tied to an anonymous app user ID.
- **Crash and performance logs** (if enabled by the platform).

We do **not** collect your name, email, contacts, location, or health data.

## 2. How we use information

- To provide core app features (logging, streaks, coaching).
- To validate and restore subscriptions.
- To diagnose crashes and improve stability.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising.

## 3. Sharing

We share limited data only with the service providers needed to run the app:
- Apple / Google (payments)
- RevenueCat (subscription management)
- Our AI inference provider (only the prompt you send to the G.O. Coach)

These providers are bound by their own privacy terms.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

Your logs and streaks remain on your device until you delete them via **Settings → Delete All Data** or uninstall the app. To request deletion of any server-side subscription record, email us at the address below.

## 5. Children

Gross Out Diet is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g., EEA, UK, California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Contact us to exercise these rights.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
